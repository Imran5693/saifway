## Google and generative AI discovery

Implemented: consistent identity copy; linked Person, Organization and WebSite entities; author identity in article/project markup; profile and service pages; crawlable internal links; server-rendered technical text; accessible diagrams with captions; existing sitemap and robots discovery.

Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode have no additional special markup requirement. The page needs to be indexed and eligible for snippets. Accurate content, internal discovery and useful supporting visuals remain relevant. There is no guarantee of crawling, indexing, ranking or citation.

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Robots.txt allows crawling generally. Hosting/CDN access and each search service's crawler behavior still need live validation. Making a page crawlable does not place it into every AI system, update a model's training data or guarantee a recommendation. No special AI text file or hidden prompt was added.

## Work that establishes authority after publishing

1. Verify Search Console ownership, check index coverage and selected canonicals, then inspect the homepage, profile, service page and guide. Submit the sitemap.
2. Use Keyword Planner for country-specific demand and a proper organic SERP assessment. Treat advertising competition separately from organic difficulty. Track Pakistan student intent separately from global business inquiries.
3. Keep LinkedIn and GitHub identity descriptions consistent with the chosen niche. Add links from authentic project repositories and professional profiles to the matching website case studies. No external profile edits were performed.
4. Add approved case evidence: sanitized code, architecture, environment versions, test method and measured outcomes. Publish testimonials only with permission. Describe labs and proposals accurately.
5. Prioritize a detailed Netmiko timing/prompt troubleshooting article, a read-only configuration audit walkthrough, and a Flask infrastructure dashboard case study based on actual implementations.
6. Track qualified inquiries alongside impressions and clicks. Review branded versus non-branded queries and landing pages in comparable 28-day windows. Test whether the service page attracts the right work, rather than judging success by generic keyword positions alone.

All changes remain local pending deployment. Account-level indexing, search-volume, Google rank tracking and live lead-delivery verification have not been performed.
