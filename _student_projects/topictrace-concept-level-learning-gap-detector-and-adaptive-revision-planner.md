## What this project covers

Use assessment evidence to identify concept-level learning gaps and produce an explained revision plan.

## Why this matches Imran’s experience

## Main modules to plan

- Map questions and assessment outcomes to concepts.
- Prepare model features and explained learning-gap alerts.
- Show revision priorities and teacher-facing analytics.

## Technology and feasibility checks

Topic-level recommendations depend on correctly tagged assessment data. XGBoost and SHAP are extensions of the demonstrated ML stack and need an initial feasibility check.

## Testing and demonstration evidence

Hold out evaluation records, review explanation stability and check that weak concepts lead to relevant revision activities rather than generic advice.
