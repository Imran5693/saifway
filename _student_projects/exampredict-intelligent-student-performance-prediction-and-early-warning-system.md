## What this project covers

Predict examination scores and compare Linear Regression with Random Forest Regression through a Flask analytics application.

## Why this matches Imran’s experience

Support fit: **Strong match**. This assessment is based on Flask REST integration and applied machine-learning projects. See the [related portfolio evidence](/projects/2026-04-24-ml-cyber-attack-detection/). This is a proposed support topic, not a claim that this exact project has already been delivered.

## Main modules to plan

- Prepare academic and study-habit inputs with a reproducible split.
- Implement prediction, model-comparison, history and metrics endpoints.
- Display estimated scores, instructor-defined risk levels and study suggestions.

## Technology and feasibility checks

The brief describes a synthetic student dataset. Results demonstrate a modelling workflow and cannot establish real student outcomes.

## Testing and demonstration evidence

Compare MAE, RMSE and R-squared on held-out data. Verify input ranges and ensure prediction uses the fitted training preprocessing pipeline.
